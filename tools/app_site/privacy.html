Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

Gotchi Apps (gotchi-apps.vercel.app) is run by Altio AI Private Limited. It lets you install apps and firmware on your Cheeko Gotchi over USB.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We use them only to let you into the site and to keep a record of who signed in. We do not ask for any other Google data.

Your Gotchi

The site talks to your Gotchi directly from your browser. Information about the device, such as its installed apps, stays in your browser and is not stored by us.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. The site is hosted on Vercel, which processes requests on our behalf.
